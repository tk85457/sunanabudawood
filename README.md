## 🌟 About Sunan Abu Dawood

**Sunan Abu Dawood (سنن أبي داود)**, compiled by **Imam Abu Dawood Sulayman ibn al-Ash'ath (rahimahullah)**, is one of the premier Hadith collections focusing primarily on legal rulings (Ahkam) and Islamic jurisprudence.

This Flutter app delivers the complete **Sunan Abu Dawood** collection with Urdu and English translations, fast offline search, and elegant dark mode.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📜 **Complete Collection**: All books and chapters of Sunan Abu Dawood.
- 🌐 **Dual Translations**: Accurate **Urdu** (سنن أبي داود اردو) and **English** translations.
- ⚡ **Offline Search**: Instant search by Hadith number or keyword.
- 📡 **100% Offline**: Embedded database with no internet dependency.
- 📌 **Bookmarks**: Save important legal Hadiths and study notes.
